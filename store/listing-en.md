## Full description (limit 4000)

**2358 / 4000 characters** (the emoji section markers count as 1 character each here; Play may count some as 2, which still leaves plenty of room)

```
Gridstone is a sleek block puzzle on a classic 8x8 board. Drag gem or wood blocks onto the grid, complete full rows and columns to shatter them, and keep the board open as long as you can. It's easy to learn and hard to put down: a relaxing brain teaser for a quick break or a long session.

💎 HOW TO PLAY
• Drag one of the 3 pieces in your tray onto the 8x8 grid. A preview shows exactly where it will land.
• Fill a complete row or column to clear it.
• When all 3 pieces are placed, 3 new ones arrive.
• The game ends when none of your pieces fits. Beat your best score!

⭐ SCORING
• Every block you place scores points.
• Combos: clear 2, 3 or more lines with one piece for much bigger points.
• Streaks: keep clearing on consecutive moves to multiply your line points up to x3.
• Clear the whole board for a big bonus.

🧩 FEATURES
• A fair piece dealer that avoids impossible sets whenever a playable set exists.
• Smooth drag and drop with snapping, a drop preview and highlighted lines that will clear.
• Satisfying shatter animations, crisp sound effects and light haptics (all can be turned off).
• Premium themes to unlock with coins you earn by playing: Jewel, Walnut, Jade & Gold, Obsidian and Marble.
• Play offline: no Wi-Fi needed. A true no wifi game for the bus, a flight, or anywhere.
• No timer and no pressure. Your game is saved automatically, so you can continue any time.
• Stuck? Watch an optional ad to reroll your pieces, or revive once per game.

🧠 A RELAXING BRAIN TEASER
Block puzzle games look simple, but every placement counts. Keep lines open, plan for the big pieces, and set up combos. If you enjoy classic block games, jewel puzzles, wood block puzzles or grid puzzles, you'll feel right at home with this offline puzzle game.

💡 TIPS
• Always leave space for a 3x3 square and a long 5-block line.
• Clearing a row and a column at once is worth far more than two single clears.
• Fill edges and corners first to keep the middle open.

ℹ️ GOOD TO KNOW
• Gridstone is free to play and shows ads. Reroll and Revive come from optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch.
• There are no in-app purchases. Coins can only be earned by playing and are only used for cosmetic themes.
• No sign-up and no account.
• Privacy policy: https://offerpk.github.io/block-puzzle/privacy.html

Download Gridstone and start clearing lines!
```
