## Full description (limit 4000)

**2126 / 4000 characters** (Python `len()`; bullets count as 1)

```
Gridstone is a calm, polished 8x8 block puzzle. Drag gem blocks onto the board, fill rows and columns, and watch them shatter. No timer and no pressure: think, place, relax.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag pieces from the tray onto the 8x8 board
• Fill a row or column to clear it; clear several at once for big combos
• Streak bonuses reward clearing on consecutive moves
• The game ends when no piece fits. Beat your best score

COME BACK EVERY DAY
• A Daily Board with the same seeded start for everyone, plus a 7-day reward calendar
• Rising coin rewards for a 7-day streak (miss a day and it restarts)
• Daily challenge streaks

WEEKLY CHALLENGE
• Three new goals every week, the same for every player
• Earn coins and collect a crest badge for each completed week, shown in your Achievements
• A gentle mid-week reminder on the Daily card (never a notification)

ACHIEVEMENTS & STATS
• 18 achievements for progress, skill, streaks, daily challenges and collecting
• Stats: games played, best results, coins earned and your longest play streak

SKIN SHOP
• 12 block themes (Jewel, Walnut, Sakura Paper, Coral Reef, Jade & Gold, Neon Arcade, Obsidian, Marble, Tide Pool, Ember Kiln, Nebula Glass, Moss & Stone) and backgrounds
• Preview before you buy. Everything is cosmetic and bought with coins you earn in the game
• No real-money purchases, no loot boxes, no cash-out

MADE FOR RELAXING
• Plays fully offline
• Friendly first-level tutorial (replay it any time from Settings)
• Sound and haptics toggles, see Accessibility below

ACCESSIBILITY
• Reduce motion (follows your phone setting, or choose yourself)
• Larger text and a high-contrast mode
• Color symbols on every piece for color-blind players
• Screen-reader support: every tray piece and board cell is labelled, and you can place pieces without dragging (select a piece, then a cell)
• Preview shop themes with these options turned on before you buy

ADS
The game is free and shows ads. Optional rewarded ads play only when you tap a button (for example a hint or a free 24-hour skin try). Full-screen ads only appear between finished games or levels, never at launch.
```
